# Streamline South site — project conventions

## Copy style

- **No em dashes in user-facing copy.** Do not use `—` (or `&mdash;`, or `--` standing in for one) in
  headings, body text, button labels, form copy, SEO strings, or any other visitor-facing text.
  Rewrite instead: split into two sentences, or use a comma, colon, parentheses, or period.
  En dashes in numeric ranges (e.g. `$750–$1,500`, `5–50 employees`) are fine.
